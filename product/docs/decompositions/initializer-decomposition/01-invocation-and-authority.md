## Dependencies

This area depends on the initialization request and any immediately supplied source material.

## Exclusions

This area does not resolve downstream implementation details or product-specific behavior.

## Unresolved decisions

## Successor work

Hand off to the framework and product foundations area after the intake boundary is established.
