## Normative requirements

- `UPG-DLT-001`: A managed-material delta shall be derived from two authoritative repo-spec initialization inventory endpoints: the material/output inventories associated with the target repository's active accepted baseline framework revision and the corresponding inventories associated with the supplying reconciliation-target framework revision.
- `UPG-DLT-002`: Stable `material_key` identity shall be the primary correspondence key between baseline and reconciliation-target inventories; raw Git path differences alone shall not establish managed-material identity or upgrade authority.
- `UPG-DLT-003`: Each material identity in the union of the two inventory endpoints shall receive exactly one deterministic directional classification applicable to the accepted inventory model: unchanged, added, modified, removed, or retargeted.
- `UPG-DLT-004`: An added classification means a managed material identity is absent from the baseline inventory and present in the reconciliation-target inventory; removed means present in baseline and absent from target; unchanged means the accepted material definition is equivalent across both endpoints.
- `UPG-DLT-005`: A modified classification means the same stable managed-material identity remains applicable but one or more authoritative material attributes or source content identities require reconciliation; a retargeted classification means stable identity is retained while its governed destination mapping changes.
- `UPG-DLT-006`: The delta shall not include arbitrary target-repository paths that are not represented within initializer-managed inventory authority, even when those paths differ from the supplying repo-spec checkout.
- `UPG-DLT-007`: Delta classification alone shall not authorize mutation; source-owned upgrade qualification, target-local managed-state checks, staging, and later validation gates may further restrict the legal reconciliation set.
