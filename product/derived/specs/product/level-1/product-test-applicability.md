## Normative requirements

- `INIT-PTA-001`: The product shall define `product-test-applicability` as the governed repository-state determination of which product implementation-test obligations are applicable to `product/scripts/test-product`.
- `INIT-PTA-002`: Product-test applicability shall be derived from accepted repository-local product authority and test obligation evidence, not inferred solely from the presence or absence of discovered test files.
- `INIT-PTA-003`: A repository may have a legitimate zero-applicable product-test state only when accepted repository-local authority establishes that no governed product implementation-test obligation applies to the current repository state.
- `INIT-PTA-004`: Legitimate zero-applicable state shall be represented by deterministic evidence sufficient to distinguish intentional absence of governed product implementation tests from accidental absence, missing registration, broken discovery, or incomplete installation.
- `INIT-PTA-005`: If accepted repository-local authority establishes that one or more governed product implementation-test obligations apply, failure to resolve all expected applicable obligations shall not be classified as zero applicable.
- `INIT-PTA-006`: The applicability state shall distinguish at minimum: zero-applicable, applicable-and-resolved, and applicability-invalid. Applicability-invalid shall include contradictory, incomplete, malformed, or otherwise insufficient governing evidence.
- `INIT-PTA-007`: Applicability-invalid shall fail closed and shall not be normalized to zero-applicable or successful product testing.
- `INIT-PTA-008`: Transition from zero-applicable to applicable product tests shall occur through governed repository authority that establishes one or more applicable product-test obligations; the stable `product/scripts/test-product` interface identity shall not change as a consequence of that transition.
- `INIT-PTA-009`: This applicability contract shall not prescribe a registry file format, discovery path layout, activation trigger representation, product-specific test semantics, test framework, or exact user-facing wording.
