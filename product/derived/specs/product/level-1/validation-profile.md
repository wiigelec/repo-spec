## Normative requirements

- `INIT-VP-001`: The validation profile shall declare a stable ordered set of checks, each with a unique check_id, classification (required or advisory), deterministic order number, applies_to scope, pass_condition, and an explicit list of failure_codes.
- `INIT-VP-002`: A required check that produces a status of failed or error shall cause overall validation to fail and shall block promotion.
- `INIT-VP-003`: An advisory check that produces a status of failed or error shall be recorded in the validation report but shall not cause overall validation to fail or block promotion.
- `INIT-VP-004`: The validation profile shall be versioned via profile_version. Successor versions may add new checks at new order numbers without renumbering existing checks. No check_id shall be removed from a published profile version; a check may be deprecated but shall remain defined.
- `INIT-VP-005`: Every check in the profile must apply to one of the following scopes: request, source, material-manifest, repository-worktree, or git-state. Checks are executed in two phases: Phase 1 (input and source, orders 10-70) and Phase 2 (staged repository and Git state, orders 80-200). Each phase executes checks in ascending order number within that phase. No ordinary check may read or validate content under transaction/; staging-state validation is not an ordinary profile check.
- `INIT-VP-006`: After all Phase 1 and Phase 2 checks are complete, report finalization is a deterministic ordered write operation that shall proceed in this exact order: (a) calculate the proposed overall_status (pass when every required Phase 1 and Phase 2 check has status passed, otherwise fail); (b) construct in memory a proposed final validation report carrying the proposed overall_status, the request fingerprint computed once by request intake under product.initialization-request::INIT-REQ-012 and carried unchanged by the validated request model, and the expected repository_content_digest from the output.repository-digest-match check; (c) construct in memory a proposed final staging-state representation carrying validation_completed equal to true, validation_overall_status equal to the proposed overall_status, the same unchanged request fingerprint, and the expected repository_content_digest; (d) validate the proposed staging-state representation against the product.staging-state schema in memory without writing it; (e) validate the proposed validation report against the validation-report schema in memory, verifying the request_fingerprint linkage, the repository_content_digest linkage, and check ordering and uniqueness; (f) serialize and write the finalized validation report atomically to transaction/validation-report.json; (g) serialize and write the finalized staging-state record atomically to transaction/staging-state.json; and (h) re-read or otherwise verify that the two durable records are mutually consistent, confirming they share the same request_fingerprint and repository_content_digest and that the staging-state validation_completed and validation_overall_status agree with the completed validation result. Report finalization shall not reserialize the request or recompute its fingerprint. These postconditions are not ordinary profile checks; they are validation-component invariants enforced during report finalization before promotion. Any failure in steps (a) through (h) shall prevent promotion.
- `INIT-VP-007`: Promotion is permitted only when both the finalized validation report and the finalized staging-state record are durably written and verified mutually consistent, the report validates against its schema, every required Phase 1 and Phase 2 check has status passed, and overall_status is pass. Promotion must not begin when only one of the two records has been durably written. Report finalization failure (staging-state schema validation failure, fingerprint mismatch, digest mismatch, validation-status mismatch, report schema validation failure, serialization failure, linkage mismatch, or failure of the final consistency verification) shall prevent promotion and shall be recorded as a pre-promotion failure with the staging transaction root preserved for diagnostics.
