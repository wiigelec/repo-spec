## Normative requirements

- `INIT-PID-001`: A product identifier shall be a stable, non-empty string that uniquely identifies a product in the bounded initialization workflow and shall be supplied as the `product.id` field of the initialization request.
- `INIT-PID-002`: The product identifier shall match the pattern `^[a-z][a-z0-9]*(-[a-z0-9]+)*$`: it shall start with a lowercase letter, contain only lowercase ASCII letters, ASCII digits, and hyphens, and hyphens shall not appear consecutively or at the end.
- `INIT-PID-003`: The product identifier shall be case-sensitive and lowercase; uppercase letters, mixed case, or non-ASCII characters shall be rejected.
- `INIT-PID-004`: The product identifier shall serve as the canonical product directory name in the initialized repository: generated product paths under `product/` shall use the identifier verbatim as the product subdirectory name.
- `INIT-PID-005`: The product identifier shall be immutable for the initialized repository: once a product is initialized with a given identifier, that identifier shall not change through subsequent initialization operations.
- `INIT-PID-006`: There shall be no separate display name for the product identifier; the identifier itself is the display name, slug, directory name, and identity — all derived values shall equal the identifier verbatim.
- `INIT-PID-007`: Two initialization requests with different product identifiers address distinct products; the initializer shall not equate, alias, or map between different identifiers.
