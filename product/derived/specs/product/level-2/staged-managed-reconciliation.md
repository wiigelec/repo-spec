## Normative requirements

- `UPG-SMR-001`: Staged managed reconciliation shall operate on an isolated staged copy or equivalent isolated staged representation of the existing target repository; the accepted target shall not be mutated while reconciliation is incomplete.
- `UPG-SMR-002`: Only material identities present in the resolved legal reconciliation set may be added, modified, removed, or retargeted by this component.
- `UPG-SMR-003`: Target content outside initializer-managed authority shall be preserved byte-for-byte or semantically unchanged according to its governing representation and shall not be deleted, replaced, or retargeted merely because the supplying framework lacks or differs at the same path.
- `UPG-SMR-004`: For selected managed material, add, modify, remove, and retarget operations shall realize the reconciliation-target inventory definition and exact source material from the supplying framework revision, subject to applicable material-manifest operation semantics.
- `UPG-SMR-005`: Initializer-managed generated or installed projections shall be reconciled consistently with the governing managed source material so the staged repository does not retain a stale managed projection after its source authority changes.
- `UPG-SMR-006`: A target-local modification to initializer-managed material that cannot be proven equivalent to the accepted baseline managed state shall be surfaced as a managed-state conflict and shall not be silently overwritten; absent a separately accepted automatic conflict policy, the reconciliation shall fail or defer before destructive replacement.
- `UPG-SMR-007`: The component shall preserve deterministic evidence of selected operations, conflicts, and resulting staged managed state for downstream re-anchoring and validation.
