## Normative requirements

- `INIT-PTL-001`: `product/scripts/test-product` shall resolve product implementation-test lifecycle state from `product-test-applicability` before reporting product-test success.
- `INIT-PTL-002`: When applicability is zero-applicable, `product/scripts/test-product` may complete successfully only after validating the deterministic evidence that establishes legitimate zero-applicable state.
- `INIT-PTL-003`: A successful zero-applicable result shall mean that no governed applicable product implementation-test obligation exists for the current repository state. It shall not mean that expected tests were unavailable, undiscovered, uninstalled, unexecutable, or skipped because of an execution failure.
- `INIT-PTL-004`: When applicability is applicable-and-resolved, `product/scripts/test-product` shall execute every governed applicable product implementation-test obligation selected by the accepted applicability evidence and shall fail if any required applicable obligation fails.
- `INIT-PTL-005`: When accepted authority indicates applicable product-test obligations but the corresponding governed tests cannot be resolved, lifecycle resolution shall fail with a distinct expected-but-undiscovered or unresolved-applicable classification rather than report zero applicable.
- `INIT-PTL-006`: Missing stable interface capability, missing required runtime dependency, broken discovery or registration processing, malformed applicability evidence, and test execution failure shall remain failure classes distinct from legitimate zero-applicable state.
- `INIT-PTL-007`: The product-test lifecycle shall produce deterministic obligation selection and execution ordering for equivalent accepted authority and repository state. The exact ordering algorithm is not prescribed by this contract.
- `INIT-PTL-008`: The lifecycle result shall provide enough machine-usable classification to distinguish successful zero-applicable, successful applicable execution, failed applicable tests, unresolved expected tests, applicability-invalid evidence, interface/dependency failure, and execution infrastructure failure. Exact serialization and diagnostic wording remain implementation-defined.
- `INIT-PTL-009`: Changing from zero-applicable to one or more applicable governed product tests shall not require redesign or repository-class-specific modification of common validation orchestration; common orchestration shall continue invoking `product/scripts/test-product`.
- `INIT-PTL-010`: This lifecycle contract shall not define concrete product test suites, exact registration or discovery architecture, activation event syntax, test runner or framework selection, exact exit-code numbering beyond success versus failure, or product-specific test semantics.
