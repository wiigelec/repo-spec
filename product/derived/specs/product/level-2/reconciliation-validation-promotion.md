## Normative requirements

- `UPG-VAP-001`: The complete re-anchored staged repository, including the prospective reconciliation-target framework anchor and prospective lineage entry in the exact serialized form intended for the accepted repository, shall undergo all repository validation required for that candidate accepted framework state before promotion may begin.
- `UPG-VAP-002`: Any required validation failure, error, unresolved managed-state conflict, inconsistent candidate anchor, or incomplete required evidence shall prevent promotion and shall leave the previously accepted target framework lineage unchanged.
- `UPG-VAP-003`: Promotion shall commit the exact complete validated staged repository state as one governed transition whose success or failure is determinable; promotion shall not require a post-validation maintained-repository content rewrite to complete the framework transition, and no partially reconciled repository may be reported as accepted success.
- `UPG-VAP-004`: If promotion successfully commits the exact validated staged repository state, the prospective reconciliation-target lineage entry contained in that state becomes an accepted lineage entry as a consequence of the committed transition and becomes the active baseline for the next reconciliation. No separate post-promotion append or other maintained-repository lineage mutation is required or authorized to establish that acceptance.
- `UPG-VAP-005`: Rejected, failed, interrupted, indeterminate, or non-promoted attempts shall not make a prospective lineage entry accepted, even when that entry and candidate re-anchoring evidence existed in staging; the previously accepted repository and accepted lineage shall remain authoritative unless promotion is known to have committed.
- `UPG-VAP-006`: Terminal evidence shall distinguish at minimum accepted promoted success from rejection before reconciliation, failure before promotion, indeterminate promotion, and promotion committed with finalization error when such an outcome is technically possible.
- `UPG-VAP-007`: Post-promotion cleanup or finalization failure shall not falsely rewrite a committed promotion as unpromoted. Because accepted lineage is established by commitment of the exact validated lineage-bearing repository state, terminal evidence shall report whether that transition committed and shall not perform a later repository-content rewrite merely to synchronize lineage acceptance.
- `UPG-VAP-008`: Automatic rollback, retry, or resume after an indeterminate promotion is not authorized by this contract and requires separate accepted semantics.
- `UPG-VAP-009`: Complete staged validation shall verify all transportable framework-authority evidence required by the prospective accepted lineage state using repository-local Git-object identity traversal. Missing, incomplete, unanchored, or tampered required authority shall prevent promotion; successful promotion accepts exactly the validated lineage and framework-authority state together.
