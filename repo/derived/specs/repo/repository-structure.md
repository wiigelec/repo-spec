## Normative requirements

- `REPO-STR-001`: The repository shall separate repository-spec source, product-spec source, schemas, derived docs, and tooling into distinct top-level areas.
- `REPO-STR-002`: The repository-specification root shall be `repo/specs/repo/`, the product-specification root shall be `product/specs/product/`, and the two roots shall remain distinct authoritative domains.
- `REPO-STR-003`: The repository shall reserve `product/specs/product/level-0/`, `product/specs/product/level-1/`, `product/specs/product/level-2/`, and `product/specs/product/level-3/` as the conceptual product-specification Level roots, along with `product/schemas/product/` and `product/derived/specs/product/`.
- `REPO-STR-004`: An initialized repository may omit product-specification files until product planning activates the product-specification system, but repository specifications shall not be placed under `product/specs/product/` and product specifications shall not be placed under `repo/specs/repo/`.
- `REPO-STR-005`: The repository manifest shall govern discovery of repository specifications only. Repository/framework governed directional and planning documents maintained by this source repository shall use `repo/docs/overview/`, `repo/docs/decompositions/`, and `repo/docs/plans/`; product-owned governed development documents maintained by this source repository shall use `product/docs/overview/`, `product/docs/decompositions/`, and `product/docs/plans/`. Initialized-repository product development-document outputs are governed separately by the accepted initializer output contracts. None of these documents becomes repository-workflow authority.
- `REPO-STR-006`: The repository shall use `repo/profiles/github/` as the populated reusable GitHub profile source location, `.github/` as the installed GitHub adapter location, and the applicable repository/framework or product-owned development-document roots as the locations for their respective top-level controlling documents and subordinate chunk directories while keeping all of them subordinate to accepted repository specifications.
- `REPO-STR-007`: Remote hosting-platform deployment state shall remain outside repository content and shall be represented only through explicit evidence or declared deployment-state records rather than ordinary generated artifacts.
- `REPO-STR-008`: The repository shall require deterministic root-index or README links to every governed development-document root so repository-local discovery does not depend on filename guessing or conversation history.
- `REPO-STR-009`: The target one-repository layout shall place reusable framework and repository material under `repo/` and product-owned material under `product/`, while preserving explicit authority and discovery boundaries between those trees.
- `REPO-STR-010`: The maintained source-repository root shall contain exactly the following governed entries: directories `.github/`, `product/`, `reference/`, `repo/`, `scripts/`, `user/`, and `validation/`; files `.gitignore`, `AGENTS.md`, `LICENSE`, and `README.md`. Root `docs/` is not a maintained source-repository namespace. No other maintained root entry is permitted without governed revision of this specification.
- `REPO-STR-011`: The `reference/` tree shall remain a separately classified reference boundary and shall not be treated as either the reusable `repo/` tree or the product-owned `product/` tree.
- `REPO-STR-012`: The repository shall reserve `user/` for non-normative user-facing convenience and session-support artifacts; content under `user/` shall remain subordinate to accepted repository and product authority and shall not establish or override repository, product, workflow, planning, validation, or implementation semantics. `user/archive/docs/` may preserve historical or reference material displaced from `repo/docs/` or `product/docs/`; such archived material shall remain non-normative and shall not satisfy current development-document discovery, relationship, lifecycle, planning, or authority requirements.
- `REPO-STR-013`: The reusable repository/framework tree shall use `repo/scripts/` as its canonical user-executable wrapper and entry-point surface and `repo/src/` as its canonical maintained implementation source root. In the final conforming state both directories shall exist, `repo/scripts/` shall contain only user-executable wrappers or entry points, and all other maintained repository/framework implementation code shall reside under `repo/src/`. A `src/` hierarchy that exists only within derived or generated output is non-governing and shall not establish, constrain, or substitute for the maintained `repo/src/` hierarchy.
- `REPO-STR-014`: The product-owned tree shall use `product/scripts/` as its canonical user-executable wrapper and entry-point surface and `product/src/` as its canonical maintained product implementation source root. In the final conforming state both directories shall exist, `product/scripts/` shall contain only user-executable wrappers or entry points, and all other maintained product implementation code other than validation implementation shall reside under `product/src/`. Product-owned validation implementation shall reside under `product/validation/`, while inherently cross-domain or whole-checkout validation shall reside under root `validation/`. A `src/` hierarchy that exists only within derived or generated output is non-governing and shall not establish, constrain, or substitute for the maintained `product/src/` hierarchy.
- `REPO-STR-015`: The maintained source-repository documentation namespaces shall be closed. `repo/docs/` shall contain exactly the direct child directories `overview/`, `decompositions/`, and `plans/`, and `product/docs/` shall contain exactly the direct child directories `overview/`, `decompositions/`, and `plans/`. No other direct file or directory is permitted beneath either documentation namespace. Material that does not conform to the governed development-document model shall be removed from these namespaces or, when preservation is useful, retained only in an authority-appropriate location outside them.
