## Purpose

## Normative requirements

- `REPO-POV-001`: A product overview shall be stored under `docs/overview/` and shall use the canonical top-level `*-OVERVIEW.md` controlling document plus subordinate chunks.
- `REPO-POV-002`: A product overview shall record product identity, problem or opportunity, intended users or stakeholders, desired outcomes, scope, non-goals, product boundaries, durable principles, major capabilities, success conditions, unresolved product direction, predecessor evidence, and readiness for decomposition, with semantic coverage separated from subordinate chunk layout. Predecessor evidence may come from discovery records, interviews, proposals, issues, statements, or other non-overview sources.
- `REPO-POV-003`: A product overview shall remain directional and non-normative and shall not define exact product behavior, accepted interfaces, accepted schemas, or release readiness.
- `REPO-POV-004`: The top-level controlling document shall act as the index, status declaration, and navigation entry point for the overview composite document.
- `REPO-POV-005`: A product overview may contain a concise summary, but the substantive directional content shall live in subordinate chunks, and a semantic area may span multiple chunks or share chunks with other areas. An initial overview may declare `overview_role: initial` instead of pointing to a prior overview, while revisions and derived overviews shall declare predecessor evidence or a prior overview lineage.
- `REPO-POV-006`: A product overview shall expose unresolved product-direction questions rather than silently resolving them in later stages.
- `REPO-POV-007`: A product overview shall declare when it is ready for decomposition, but that readiness shall remain directional rather than constituting acceptance of normative behavior.
